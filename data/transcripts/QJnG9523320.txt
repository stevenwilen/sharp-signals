Edson Bambosa is no walk in the park. If
you look at his resume, he's fought the
who's who of the division. I agree with
the retirement. I think it's probably
one of those things where it's like you
got to be honest with yourself and your
abilities and and see how much damage
you're willing to continue to keep
taking. He's given us a lot of
highlights. He's given us a lot of fun
fights. I remember his fight with Dan
Hooker, the crushing body shot that
finally put Dan the zombie down and the
last kick he threw to the body and Dan
folded. Edson has had a great career.
He's fought Kabib. He's fought Kevin
Lee. Everything he's been able to do so
far has been nothing short of
entertaining. But not everybody gets
that final dance and it goes the way
that they wanted to go. Great career,
great fight.
